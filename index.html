Research Focus

Questions I'm chasing.

Fundamental inquiries across reasoning architectures, evaluation games, and scientific discovery.

INQUIRY 01 · MULTI-AGENT REASONING

How can multiple agents collaborate to produce more reliable reasoning?

Investigating decentralized multi-agent topologies where specialized agents debate, critique, and cross-verify intermediate reasoning steps to mitigate hallucination and eliminate single-model failure modes.

INQUIRY 02 · LLM EVALUATION

How can LLM judges and evaluators be made verifiable and non-gameable?

Exploring game-theoretic mechanism design, reference-free verification, and calibration strategies to counter positional bias, verbosity bias, and self-enhancement in automated LLM evaluation.

INQUIRY 03 · SCIENTIFIC ML

How can physical and dynamical invariants be recovered from learned representations?

Combining neural differential equations with sparse regression (SINDy) and autograd derivative extraction to rediscover interpretable, symbolic governing equations directly from noisy experimental trajectories.

INQUIRY 04 · KNOWLEDGE & MEMORY

How can personal knowledge graphs evolve dynamically over time?

Developing memory decay, conceptual association algorithms, and conversational retrieval systems (as explored in The Mirror) that model human memory retention and serendipitous idea synthesis.
