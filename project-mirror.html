Featured Project Deep Dive

The Mirror

“Your thoughts, reflected back to you.” An AI-powered interactive visualization system that transforms unstructured thoughts, notes, journals, and ideas into a living planetarium of an intellectual world.

SECTION 01

Overview

Human thought is non-linear, multi-modal, and fluid. We record insights in fragmented journal entries, brief audio snippets, captured images, and half-formed notes. Yet, conventional note-taking platforms force this dynamic cognition into static hierarchical trees or chronological feeds where ideas inevitably wither away.

The Mirror re-imagines personal knowledge as an active, living celestial system. Concepts exist as luminous stars whose gravitational attraction reflects semantic affinity. Clusters form galaxies of interest, while temporal scrubbers allow users to witness the emergence, branching, and decay of their thoughts over days, months, and years.

SECTION 02

The Problem

Digital knowledge capture suffers from three persistent failure modes:

ISSUE 01

The Flat-File Cemetery

When notes are organized in folders or flat files, finding relationships between disparate concepts requires manual linking. As the archive grows, older insights are buried and rarely revisited.

ISSUE 02

Lack of Temporal Context

Most tools treat thoughts as timeless text strings, stripping away the context of how an intellectual passion blossomed, migrated into an adjacent domain, or cooled off over time.

ISSUE 03

Sensory Disconnection

Thoughts are frequently anchored to sensory cues—a voice recording made during a late-night walk, or a photograph of a whiteboard diagram. Standard note apps strip away this multi-modal resonance.

ISSUE 04

Absence of Reflective Feedback

Traditional note archives are passive repositories. They cannot talk back, reveal hidden contradictions, or prompt the user with forgotten hypotheses.

SECTION 03

The Approach

The Mirror approaches personal knowledge through the metaphor of an evolving celestial planetarium:

1. Semantic Gravitational Physics: Nodes representing concepts are subject to physical force simulation. Concepts that share conceptual relationships, mutual references, or thematic overlaps attract each other, forming organic solar systems and constellations.

2. Multi-Modal Anchoring: Users attach photographic visual anchors and voice memos directly to nodes, creating rich, sensory-anchored landmarks within the constellation.

3. Living Evolution & Decay Dynamics: Nodes possess timestamps and interaction metrics. Fresh concepts burn with high luminance, while older unvisited nodes gently dim, simulating human memory decay until serendipitously reignited by new queries.

SECTION 04

Architecture & Core Systems

The system is decomposed into specialized interactive subsystems:

FEATURE 01

Galaxy Thought Map

A high-performance HTML5 Canvas 2D spatial engine that renders hundreds of interconnected thoughts with 60fps force simulation, smooth panning, and multi-level zoom.

FEATURE 02

Visual Memory Anchors

Attach photographic memory anchors to concepts. Clicking an anchor opens a visual gallery directly within the spatial canvas context.

FEATURE 03

Voice Thoughts

Record audio voice reflections directly against specific nodes. Audio can be replayed and inspected in-situ without leaving the constellation.

FEATURE 04

Temporal Evolution

A dedicated time scrubber and timeline player allowing users to slide through time and observe how ideas were born, clustered, and evolved chronologically.

FEATURE 05

Concept Inspector

An in-depth slide-out panel that parses node details, extracted entities, incoming/outgoing connections, and historical modification logs.

FEATURE 06

Hidden Connections

An AI discovery module that computes latent semantic proximity between distant clusters, highlighting non-obvious cross-domain links.

FEATURE 07

Forgotten Ideas Engine

A resurfacing algorithm that detects dormant nodes that haven't been visited or updated, presenting them as prompts for new contemplation.

FEATURE 08

Ask the Mirror

A conversational reflection agent powered by Claude Sonnet that interrogates the user's graph, answering queries and synthesizing synthesis reports.

FEATURE 09

Share Thought Poster

Generates high-resolution graphical posters of selected constellations and subgraphs, perfect for sharing intellectual roadmaps.

FEATURE 10

Interactive Demo Mode

A built-in curated walkthrough loaded with sample intellectual universes to experience every feature without entering private data.
