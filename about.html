Identity & Background

Research-minded.
Builder at heart.

I explore the intersection of machine intelligence, reasoning architectures, and scientific discovery — grounding foundational math in working systems.

01 / MY DIRECTION

Moving beyond static inference toward verifiable reasoning

I am currently an undergraduate student in Data Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Bhopal. My core intellectual interests center on Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Systems, LLM Reasoning & Evaluation, and Scientific Machine Learning.

Standard autoregressive inference often produces fluent text without genuine epistemological grounding. My work investigates how autonomous agents can coordinate, use external tools, verify intermediate computation, and deliberate to solve non-trivial reasoning challenges reliably.

02 / HOW I BUILD

From mathematical abstraction to production-grade implementations

I believe that profound insights in AI stem from writing code from first principles. Whether it means implementing Transformer multi-head attention mechanisms from scratch in PyTorch, extracting autograd derivatives to rediscover nonlinear differential equations, or building interactive canvas-based cognitive maps like The Mirror, I build systems end-to-end.

I enjoy going from paper formulation to experimental ablation — probing architecture boundaries, benchmarking against strong baselines, and analyzing failure modes.

Guiding Principles

How I approach problems.

Three core beliefs that guide every codebase, research question, and interface I develop.

PRINCIPLE 01

First-Principles Construction

Do not rely solely on opaque black-box libraries until you understand the underlying mathematics. Implementing architectures from scratch builds intuition for gradients, tensor mechanics, and computational bottlenecks.

PRINCIPLE 02

Empirical Skepticism

A high metric without thorough ablation is a deceptive victory. Every experiment must feature controlled baselines, clear hyperparameter tracking, and honest error analysis.

PRINCIPLE 03

Interpretability & Human Agency

AI systems should expand human cognition rather than obscure it. I focus on making representations inspectable, visualizable, and directly interactable for human thinkers.

Interdisciplinary Research Culture

At the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal, science is approached holistically. The Data Science & Engineering curriculum synthesizes rigorous mathematical foundations (advanced linear algebra, probability theory, multivariable calculus, differential equations) with computational sciences, algorithms, and applied machine learning.

This environment fosters a scientific mindset: questioning assumptions, prioritizing reproducibility, and treating computation as an empirical laboratory.
